French Toast

Category: Breakfast

Easy 10 min
(4.4)

A delicious breakfast classic that's easy to make. Sweet, warm, and satisfying - the perfect way to start your day!

Dietary Information:
Vegetarian

Ingredients

  • 4 slices bread (preferably slightly stale)
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • Maple syrup (for serving)
  • Fresh berries (optional, for serving)

Instructions

Preparation
  1. In a shallow bowl, whisk together eggs, milk, vanilla extract, and cinnamon until well combined.
  2. Heat butter in a large skillet over medium heat until melted.
  3. Dip each slice of bread into the egg mixture, allowing it to soak for a few seconds on each side.
  4. Place the soaked bread in the hot skillet and cook until golden brown on both sides, about 2-3 minutes per side.
  5. Transfer to serving plates and serve warm with maple syrup and fresh berries if desired.
Tips
  • For extra flavor, add a pinch of nutmeg to the egg mixture.
  • Slightly stale bread works best as it absorbs the egg mixture without becoming too soggy.
