Official CookOff Rules & Guidelines

Our live cooking competitions are fun, fair, and focused on creativity. Please review the following rules before participating.

General Rules

Time Limits

Each CookOff challenge has a strict time limit, typically 30 minutes. When time is called, all cooking must stop immediately. The timer begins once both participants have confirmed they are ready.

Equipment & Ingredients

Participants must use their own equipment and ingredients. You should prepare your workspace before joining the CookOff. Daily challenges will be announced 24 hours in advance to allow for shopping.

Video Requirements

Your camera must show your cooking area clearly. Face must be visible throughout the challenge. Maintain good lighting and keep your microphone on to interact with your opponent and spectators.

Judging & Voting

Winners are determined by viewer votes. At the end of cooking, both participants must present their completed dish to the camera. Viewers will vote based on appearance, creativity, and perceived tastiness.

Participation Guidelines

Do's:

  • Be respectful to your opponent and spectators
  • Explain your process as you cook
  • Be creative and have fun
  • Share tips and techniques
  • Practice food safety and hygiene
  • Present your dish clearly at the end

Don'ts:

  • Use pre-made or pre-cooked main components
  • Leave the camera frame for extended periods
  • Use inappropriate language or behavior
  • Criticize your opponent's techniques
  • Have someone else cook for you
  • Use unsafe cooking practices

Technical Requirements

  • Internet Connection: Stable broadband connection with minimum 5Mbps upload speed
  • Camera: HD webcam or smartphone camera capable of 720p resolution
  • Audio: Clear microphone, preferably with background noise reduction
  • Lighting: Well-lit cooking area without harsh shadows or glare
  • Browser: Latest version of Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
  • Device: Computer, laptop or tablet with webcam support (mobile phones not recommended)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use pre-chopped vegetables?

Yes, basic prep work like washing and chopping vegetables is allowed, but main components should be prepared during the challenge. For example, you can have chopped onions ready, but you can't bring a pre-made sauce.

What happens if I lose my internet connection?

If you lose connection for less than 2 minutes, the competition will continue. For longer disruptions, we may reschedule your match or determine a result based on progress made up to that point.

Can I substitute ingredients in the challenge?

Yes! Creativity is encouraged. While the challenge will specify a main dish or concept, you're free to interpret it in your own way using ingredients you have available.

How are winners determined?

Winners are determined by audience voting during the live stream. Viewers can vote for their favorite dish based on presentation, creativity, and perceived tastiness. The chef with the most votes at the end of the voting period wins.

Are there prizes for winning?

Winners receive points that contribute to their ranking on our leaderboard. Monthly top performers receive badges, featured status, and occasionally physical prizes like cooking equipment or gift cards from our sponsors.
